About Me

Early Life

I was born on the first of January 2000, the first of four children. I grew up relatively quietly and peacefully, well in comparison to other childhoods I've heard of. I really didn't have to struggle with much and things went generally smoothly. There were a few life changing decisions my parents took, like changing my school and our church, but besides that, nothing interesting really happened in my childhood.

Teenage Years

My teenage years were also pretty similar to my early years. Middle school came by and left without significant problems and besides one change in my life that resulted in me having to attend a different high school than I'd intended to nothing else really happened. I finished high school successfully and was accepted to study computer science at Saarland University. It's been a fairly great 23 years of my life.

Adulthood
